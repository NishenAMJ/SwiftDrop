One account, three ways to use it

Everyone starts with the same account. Merchants and couriers then apply, and SwiftDrop reviews each application before it is approved.

  • Customers

    Book parcel deliveries, choose standard or urgent service, and see the fee before you confirm. Follow every booking from your dashboard.

    Create a customer account

  • Merchants

    Apply for a merchant account. Once approved, connect your online store or business system with API keys so it can send orders to SwiftDrop.

    Get started as a merchant

  • Couriers

    Apply to deliver with SwiftDrop. Once approved, see the parcels assigned to you and update their delivery progress as you go.

    Get started as a courier
